    their implications for brand visibility measurement and for the emerging practice of Generative Engine Optimization.

    commodity hardware; (b) empirical evidence on the relative contribution of fine-tuning vs. retrieval grounding in that setting; and (c) a
    discussion of the implications for AI-assisted financial inclusion in Sub-Saharan Africa.
