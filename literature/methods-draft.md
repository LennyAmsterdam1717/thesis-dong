## Notes for the full-text methods text (to include)

- Full text (go light): single-reviewer check by Claude, disclosed as such.
- Proof of reading: every full-text decision had to include a paraphrase (≤ 20 words) of a point from the limitations, discussion or conclusion with its page marker, located in the last third of the report (before the references); decisions failing this check were re-run up to twice, and any that still failed were flagged for Frank and not counted. Report how many decisions were re-run and how many were flagged.
- Search: v1.0 strings exceeded the volume ceiling and were tightened to v1.1 (concept terms in the title for R1a, R2b, R3, R4a); state the sensitivity trade-off and the mitigations (citation chasing, Google Scholar cross-check).

- Corpus size: v1.1 gave 1,854 unique records, too many for the review's purpose (2–3 RQs). Frank chose to narrow it (v1.2): an LLM term was required in the title for R1b, R2a, R2b and R4a; R2a also had to mention uncertainty, consistency or risk; R1a records had to mention knowledge or finance and R3 records uncertainty or finance; citation chasing (R4b) stayed complete. The v1.2 search found 788 database records and 65 records by citation chasing; after removing 184 duplicates, 669 records were screened. State the trade-off: title-level and cross-concept filters favour precision over sensitivity, so the corpus is a focused sample centred on the links between LLM certainty, knowledge and finance, not a complete map of each field; general methods papers are covered as background. The seed paper was retrieved by the search itself.
- **Deviation – no human calibration pilot.** The review author decided not to label a calibration pilot. Title/abstract screening was started without a passing human-labelled pilot, using SR-Screener's recorded pilot override (reason recorded in `sr_work/pilot_override.json`). Instead, a post-hoc verification sample was checked by the author: 20 randomly drawn exclusions and 10 randomly drawn inclusions, each with the AI's one-line reason, marked agree/disagree. **Update 2026-11-20: the author delegated this check to a further AI model (Claude Opus 5.5, main session), so it is an independent AI check, not a human verification.** A first marking of the same sheet by the author, which disagreed with the AI decision on 18 of 30 records, was not used. Agreement: 28/30 (93%); exclusions 18/20; inclusions 10/10. Disagreements: R00334 (LLM investment advice mentioning portfolio risk; protocol exception (a) → unclear) and R00615 (no abstract; LLMs and linguistic diversity; case (d) → unclear). No screening decision in this review has been verified by a human; this must be stated as a limitation unless the author later checks a sample himself. (The first batch had been formed as a stratified mixed sample of 25 records for the planned pilot; it was screened as a normal batch.)

- **Title/abstract screening (results).** 669 records were screened in 15 batches by two blinded AI reviewers (Claude Sonnet via the `academic-research-skills:screening-reviewer-agent`, SR-Screener 1.0.0, ARS v3.24.3) with different personas (content expert; review methodologist), each applying the confirmed protocol (v1.0) verbatim. Agreement on advance vs exclude: 97.0 % observed, Cohen's κ = 0.939, PABAK = 0.94 (n = 669; 20 conflicts). Conflicts were resolved by a third AI reviewer (adjudicator; 20 records). A senior AI reviewer, blind to earlier decisions, rechecked a reproducible random sample of 100 joint exclusions plus all exclusions matching the near-miss keyword groups (307 records in total) and advanced 7 of them. Result before the overrides described below: 197 included and 96 unclear (293 advanced), 376 excluded (publication type 81; not about LLMs 35; finance without uncertainty/knowledge angle 71; neither concept studied 187; other 2). The seed paper was included by both reviewers. PRISMA identification: databases 788 (arXiv 275, OpenAlex 513), citation searching 65; duplicates removed 184.
- Verification sample: see the deviation note above (20 exclusions + 10 inclusions, checked by Claude Opus 5.5 on the author's behalf; 28/30 agree). No human verification of screening decisions. The full automatic methods text is in `literature/screening/Screening_TA/TA_methods_selection.md`; note that the pilot-override reason recorded there (and in `sr_work/pilot_override.json`) still says "post-hoc human verification sample" — that was the plan at the time; the check was in the end done by an AI model.
- **Overrides after verification (15).** Two exclusions with which the verification check disagreed (R00334, R00615) were advanced on the author's decision. The author then asked for a recheck of protocol exception (a) and case (d); **13 further records were advanced as AI judgments by Claude Opus 5.5 (single reviewer), not by the author** (7 records without an abstract, 6 finance records evaluating the risk, reliability or consistency of the LLM's own advice). SR-Screener reports all 15 as "human overrides"; this label is incorrect for 13 of them, and the `by` column of `overrides.csv` and the screening log name the actual decider. Final title/abstract result after overrides: 197 include + 111 unclear = **308 advanced**, **361 excluded**.
- **Deviation – no full-text screening of all advanced records.** On the author's decision, the 308 advanced records were charted from title and abstract only (keyword-assisted; `charting.csv`). A single-reviewer full-text check (Claude Opus 5.5, go light) and a citation check were done only for the papers used as evidence for the gaps, research questions and supervisor email: 38 sought, 5 not retrieved (reported as "not retrieved", not excluded), 33 assessed, 30 included, 3 excluded (position paper without analysis; creative-output homogeneity; results from a simulated pilot). The other advanced records are not full-text assessed and are not reported as included studies. Proof of reading: 33/33 decisions passed at the first attempt; none was re-run or flagged.
- **Gap-claim cross-check (R5, Google Scholar).** Eight Scholar queries (first results page each) tested every "no study does X" claim. Records found there entered through the "other methods" branch: 4 screened in at title/abstract (single reviewer), 3 full-text checked and citation-checked and added to the evidence set (now 33 papers; across both rounds 36 full-text decisions, all passing the proof-of-reading check at the first attempt), 1 not retrieved; pre-2025 hits went to background (E1). Two gap claims were narrowed, one partly fell for the general domain, one was withdrawn (details in `research-questions/scholar-check.md`). The Scholar check also exposed a sensitivity cost of the v1.1 title-term tightening: one in-range paper (ACL 2025) was missed because its title used "Poor Calibration" rather than a listed phrase.
- Citation check: every evidence paper (33) was checked against the arXiv API and the DOI resolver (ARS plugin clients with a 0.70 title cross-check, plus metadata via doi.org); all passed.
