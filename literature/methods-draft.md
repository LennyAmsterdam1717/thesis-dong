- **Title/abstract screening (results).** 669 records were screened in 15 batches by two blinded AI reviewers (Claude Sonnet via the `academic-research-skills:screening-reviewer-agent`, SR-Screener 1.0.0, ARS v3.24.3) with different personas (content expert; review methodologist), each applying the confirmed protocol (v1.0) verbatim. Agreement on advance vs exclude: 97.0 % observed, Cohen's κ = 0.939, PABAK = 0.94 (n = 669; 20 conflicts). Conflicts were resolved by a third AI reviewer (adjudicator; 20 records). A senior AI reviewer, blind to earlier decisions, rechecked a reproducible random sample of 100 joint exclusions plus all exclusions matching the near-miss keyword groups (307 records in total) and advanced 7 of them. Final: 197 included and 96 unclear (293 advanced to full text), 376 excluded (publication type 81; not about LLMs 35; finance without uncertainty/knowledge angle 71; neither concept studied 187; other 2). The seed paper was included by both reviewers. PRISMA identification: databases 788 (arXiv 275, OpenAlex 513), citation searching 65; duplicates removed 184.
