# On Epistemic Diversity in Large Language Models

**Elisabeth Kirsten et al. (2026)** · COLM 2026 · peer-reviewed · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04835
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** What does it mean for an LLM to be epistemically diverse?
- **Method:** Framework: diversity as coverage of valid answers, explanations and reasoning routes; operationalised on ten models and two datasets.
- **Main finding:** Frontier LLMs are often epistemically narrow even when many valid alternatives exist.
- **Limitations:** Framework-heavy; two datasets.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G4: narrowness over valid answers - directly relevant to multiple valid financial recommendations. COLM 2026.
