# Epistemic diversity across language models mitigates knowledge collapse

**Damian Hodel et al. (2025)** · arXiv preprint · preprint · https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15011
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** Is one dominant model or a diverse ecosystem better against knowledge collapse?
- **Method:** Simulation of self-training ecosystems with segmented data across model families and sizes.
- **Main finding:** Optimal model diversity grows with the number of self-training iterations; diverse community models beat a single model over longer horizons.
- **Limitations:** Simulation, not deployed systems.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G4/T6: diversity as a protective property; background for homogeneity risk.
