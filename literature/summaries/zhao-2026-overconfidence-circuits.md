# Wired for Overconfidence: A Mechanistic Perspective on Inflated Verbalized Confidence in LLMs

**Tianyi Zhao et al. (2026)** · COLM 2026 · peer-reviewed · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01457
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** What inside the model causes inflated verbalized confidence?
- **Method:** Circuit-level mechanistic analysis; identifies 'Confidence Mover Circuits'; inference-time intervention.
- **Main finding:** Compact circuits causally drive overconfidence, are conserved across datasets, and targeted interventions improve calibration.
- **Limitations:** Open-weight models only.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G5/G2: overconfidence is a stable internal mechanism. COLM 2026.
