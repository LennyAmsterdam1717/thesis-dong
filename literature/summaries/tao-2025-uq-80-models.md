# Revisiting Uncertainty Estimation and Calibration of Large Language Models

**Linwei Tao et al. (2025)** · arXiv preprint · preprint · https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23854
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** How do uncertainty estimation and calibration vary across many LLMs?
- **Method:** 80 models (open/closed, dense/MoE, reasoning, quantised, 0.6B-671B), three black-box methods.
- **Main finding:** Model properties strongly shape uncertainty behaviour; linguistic verbal uncertainty works well; accuracy is not a proxy for uncertainty quality.
- **Limitations:** General-domain QA.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G6/T1: large reference evaluation; no finance.
