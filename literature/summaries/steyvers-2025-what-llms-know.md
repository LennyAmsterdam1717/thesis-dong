# What large language models know and what people think they know

**Mark Steyvers et al. (2025)** · Nature Machine Intelligence 7 (2025) 221-231 · peer-reviewed · https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-024-00976-7
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** Do users perceive LLM confidence correctly?
- **Method:** Experiments comparing LLM internal confidence with users' confidence in LLM answers; explanations varied by confidence.
- **Main finding:** With default explanations, users perceive LLM knowledge differently from what the model's confidence indicates; explanations tailored to model confidence (from "not sure" to "confident") narrow this gap.
- **Limitations:** Multiple-choice tasks.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G5 (key): calibration gap between model and user. Nature MI.
