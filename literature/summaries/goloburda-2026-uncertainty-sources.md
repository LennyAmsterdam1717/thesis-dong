# Why Don't You Know? Evaluating the Impact of Uncertainty Sources on Uncertainty Quantification in LLMs

**Maiya Goloburda et al. (2026)** · arXiv preprint · preprint · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10495
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** How do different sources of uncertainty affect UQ methods?
- **Method:** Controlled dataset separating knowledge gaps, output variability and input ambiguity; evaluation of UQ methods on five LLMs.
- **Main finding:** Methods work when lack of knowledge is the only source, but output variability inflates and input ambiguity deflates uncertainty estimates.
- **Limitations:** QA only; no reasoning models.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G3 (key): legitimate answer variety (as in advice) is mistaken for uncertainty.
