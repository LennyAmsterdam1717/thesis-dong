# Mind the Confidence Gap: Overconfidence, Calibration, and Distractor Effects in Large Language Models

**Prateek Chhikara et al. (2025)** · Transactions on Machine Learning Research (12/2025) · peer-reviewed · https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11028
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** How miscalibrated are LLMs on factual QA, and do distractors help?
- **Method:** Nine LLMs, three factual QA datasets, free generation vs distractor-augmented prompts.
- **Main finding:** Overconfidence is widespread; distractors improve calibration especially for small models; large models can be worse calibrated on simple questions.
- **Limitations:** QA setting only.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G5: baseline evidence of overconfidence. TMLR.
