# Adapting Large Language Models for Low-Resource Regulated Domains: A Fine-Tuning and Retrieval-Augmented Generation Approach to Insurance Information Delivery in Kenya

**AMOS MBEKI NYAGAR et al. (2026)** · preprint (Research Square) · preprint · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9770645/v1
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** Can a mid-sized open LLM be adapted to answer regulated insurance/financial questions in Kenya?
- **Method:** LLaMA-2 with LoRA fine-tuning and/or RAG on a small curated QA corpus; automatic metrics.
- **Main finding:** RAG delivered most of the gain because it injected specific regulatory facts; fine-tuning alone was largely cosmetic and reduced relevancy by encouraging confident, under-grounded answers.
- **Limitations:** Preprint; one model, small corpus, no human evaluation panel.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G2 (finance, weak): fine-tuning changes confidence/style more than knowledge.
