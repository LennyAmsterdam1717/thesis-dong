# How Do Uncertainty Estimators Align with Hallucination Occurrence in LLMs?

**Yedidia Agnimo et al. (2026)** · arXiv preprint · preprint · https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27016
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** Do uncertainty estimators reliably flag hallucinations?
- **Method:** 46 estimators across intrinsic and extrinsic hallucination settings on small open models.
- **Main finding:** Uncertainty is informative only conditionally; task-specific targets drive results; no universal hallucination detector.
- **Limitations:** Small open-weight models.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G6/T1: argues for task-specific (e.g. finance) evaluation.
