# Who Is Sycophantic to Whom? Confident LLMs, Human Deference, and the Collapse of Trust Calibration

**Deepthika S et al. (2026)** · arXiv preprint · preprint · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31579
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** When an LLM is confident, consistent and wrong, do users defer to it?
- **Method:** Within-subjects experiment, 32 participants learning science topics from five GPT-5-mini assistants with 0-100% planted falsehoods.
- **Main finding:** Learners absorbed about two in three falsehoods regardless of assistant reliability; their own confidence stopped separating right from wrong.
- **Limitations:** n = 32; science topics; preprint.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G5: strong user-side effect; finance replication is the gap.
