# Humans overrely on overconfident language models, across languages

**Neil Rathi et al. (2025)** · COLM 2025 · peer-reviewed · https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06306
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** Are LLMs overconfident across languages, and do users over-rely?
- **Method:** Analysis of epistemic markers across several languages plus user studies of reliance.
- **Main finding:** LLMs are linguistically overconfident in all languages; users over-rely everywhere; in Japanese, more uncertainty markers did not reduce reliance.
- **Limitations:** General-knowledge tasks.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G5: user side of expressed confidence; language matters (Dutch/EU context). COLM 2025.
