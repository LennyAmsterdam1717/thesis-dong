# Competing Biases underlie Overconfidence and Underconfidence in LLMs

**Dharshan Kumaran et al. (2026)** · Nature Machine Intelligence 8 (2026) 614-627 · peer-reviewed · https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01217-9
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** Why do LLMs both stick to their answers and over-react to contrary advice?
- **Method:** Two-turn experiments with advice from another LLM of stated accuracy; answer shown vs hidden; several datasets.
- **Main finding:** LLMs show a choice-supportive bias (keep their own visible answer) and over-weight contradictory advice; the bias depends on recognising the answer as their own, not on copying.
- **Limitations:** Controlled lab-style tasks.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G5: confidence dynamics matter for multi-turn advice conversations. Nature MI.
