# Evaluation Dimension Determines Conclusion: A Systematic Study of SFT and CPT Effects on Institutional Financial Knowledge Injection

**Yahua Ruan et al. (2026)** · repository report (Zenodo) · preprint · https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20972215
_Paraphrased note by Claude (single reviewer) from the abstract, introduction and discussion/limitations; full text checked 2026-11-20._

- **Question:** Which training route injects institution-specific financial knowledge: SFT or continued pre-training?
- **Method:** Eight training runs (3 SFT, 5 CPT) on Qwen3-8B; 290 probes across exact values, natural business questions and general finance.
- **Main finding:** CPT improved concept/process understanding but not exact-value recall; conclusions depend on which evaluation dimension is used.
- **Limitations:** Single-author repository report; one model; companion-benchmark dependent.
- **Relevance for this thesis:** G2 (finance, weak): acquisition route matters, and evaluation choice decides the verdict.
