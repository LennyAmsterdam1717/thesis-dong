# RQ shortlist

_v0.1, 2026-11-20. Ranking by Claude for Frank to decide; subject to the Google Scholar cross-check (`scholar-check.md`)._

1. **RQ1 – Acquisition route and confidence (main candidate).** Links both directions of the thesis (how sure LLMs are × how they acquire knowledge), which is the topic's stated bridge; strongest evidence base (peer-reviewed: ni, miao, li; plus xia, kissling); fully controlled experiments on open models, no human participants, feasible in 8 months on modest compute; fits Dong Nguyen's knowledge/epistemic line (seed paper). Risk: needs care to make injected facts realistic and to avoid contamination.
2. **RQ2 – Confidence on facts that change.** Most directly financial and practical; yields a reusable time-stamped benchmark (G6); feasible with public data. Could be merged into RQ1 as "time since acquisition" if a single RQ is preferred. Risk: evidence for the finance side partly rests on a pilot (martins); drift work exists in general domain (elbadry), so the contribution is the financial, calibration-scored version.
3. **RQ3 – Uncertainty for advice.** Highest novelty, but defining acceptable advice is a methodological risk; best kept as a stretch goal or follow-up.

Not shortlisted: **RQ4** (user study) — valuable but needs ethics approval and participant budget, and sits closer to HCI; could be a small add-on to RQ1/RQ2 if time allows.
