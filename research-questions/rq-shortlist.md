# RQ shortlist

_v0.2, 2026-11-20, after the Google Scholar cross-check. Ranking by Claude; Frank decides._

1. **RQ1 – Acquisition route and confidence on financial facts (main candidate).** Still the thesis's bridge between "how sure" and "how knowledge is acquired". After the Scholar check its novelty is narrower but clear: prior work examines one route at a time or reports accuracy (wang-2025, Gekhman 2024, Ovadia 2024); RQ1 compares several routes on the same financial facts and measures token-level and verbalized confidence. Strong evidence base, controlled experiments, no participants, feasible in 8 months. Risk: realistic fact construction and contamination control.
2. **RQ2 – Confidence on financial facts that change.** Narrowed to the confidence/calibration dimension that FinCutBench (accuracy only) leaves open; can reuse FinCutBench questions or public dated facts. Natural extension of RQ1 ("time since acquisition") if a single RQ is preferred.
3. **RQ3 – Uncertainty for advice (stretch).** Gap holds, but defining acceptable advice is a methodological risk.

Dropped after the Scholar check: RQ4 (user side of expressed confidence in finance) and a stand-alone G4 RQ.
